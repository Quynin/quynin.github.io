Tower of Hanoi

The Tower of Hanoi is a classic CS game. I've simulated it in JS here!

Objective:

Your goal is to move all of the rings from one tower to another.
At any point, you can only move the top-most ring on each tower.
Rings may only go atop bigger rings.
